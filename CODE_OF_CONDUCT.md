# Code of conduct

Runne welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces maintained for the project, as well as public representation of the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Respect privacy, credit others' work, and accept reasonable moderation decisions. Make room for questions and disagreement without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private communications or personal information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns

For a non-confidential moderation request, mention [@ryltsevmikhail](https://github.com/ryltsevmikhail) in the relevant discussion without repeating harmful or private material.

For a confidential conduct concern, use the repository's [private GitHub reporting form](https://github.com/ryltsevmikhail/Runne/security/advisories/new), with the title **Confidential conduct report**. This is the project's designated private fallback for conduct reports as well as its security-reporting channel; it requires a GitHub account and enabled private reporting. Provide relevant links, a concise description, and any immediate safety concern. Do not include unnecessary personal data.

If the form is unavailable, ask the maintainer in a non-sensitive issue to enable private reporting; do not publish the underlying details. The project does not advertise an alternate confidential email address. This channel goes to repository maintainers, not an independent ombudsperson.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude someone for serious or repeated violations. They consider severity, context, and recurrence. Reports are handled as privately as practical, but absolute confidentiality and response times cannot be guaranteed.

A person involved in a report should not adjudicate their own conduct. Requests to reconsider a moderation decision may be submitted through the same reporting channel; independent review is not guaranteed when no uninvolved maintainer is available.
